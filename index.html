Getting started

  1. Press power and allow the microphone. Check that sound card at the top shows your sound card or mic. The recorder plays five clicks and measures its own delay.
  2. Press record and play. Press stop when you are done.
  3. Press to start. Switch track 1 off and track 2 on with the numbered buttons, and add the next part. What you already recorded plays while you perform.
  4. To record several tracks at once, switch on more than one numbered button. The small menu above each number chooses which sound card input that track records from.
  5. Turn echo, reverb and pan to taste. Press mix down to wav or mix down to mp3 when it sounds right.

Keys: space play and stop, R record, 1–4 choose track, Enter to start. Double-click a knob to reset it.

The tape is kept in this browser between sessions. Wear headphones when you overdub, so the old tracks do not leak into the new one.

Sound settings

not measured

Sync is measured with five clicks from the speakers into the mic. With headphones, hold one earcup to the mic. With a guitar straight into the sound card, patch a cable from output to input while you measure.

Switch on to see what the browser is doing with your sound.

Throw away the recording?

All four tracks and the song title are erased and the tape starts empty. This cannot be undone.
